Property Walk Recorder Quick Reference Guide

The field checklist for starting, recording, reviewing, resuming, and safely uploading a walk.

Build 22 Field Workflow

  • START: Select the registered property, evaluator, Process and Visit Type; for unregistered captures enter the real address or location.
  • VERIFY: Speak and confirm the live microphone meter shows narration is being captured.
  • ROOM: Tap the current area before narrating or photographing. Use Bedroom 1–3, Bathroom 1–2, Roof, or Other for a custom room.
  • NARRATE: State the address, area, visible condition, uncertainty, correction, and required follow-up.
  • PHOTO: Use the in-app camera; narration continues while the camera is open.
  • PAUSE: Pause only for a real break; resume and reverify the microphone.
  • REVIEW: Check narration, photographs, and room labels. Review Walk saves locally but does not upload.
  • RESUME: Add missing evidence to the same Walk ID and review again.
  • DESTINATION: Confirm the registered folder, change property, paste a Drive-folder link, or use Recovery Inbox.
  • FINISH: Confirm End Walk & Upload only when evidence is complete; keep the Recorder open.
  • PROCESS: Finish only when status reads Uploaded & Processed.

Build 22 Recovery

  • Interrupted: Open My Walks and choose Continue Walk or Review Captured.
  • Upload stopped: Reconnect and choose Retry Upload; completed items are not duplicated.
  • Processing stopped: If Drive upload is safe, choose Retry Processing, not Retry Upload.
  • Folder missing: Change property, use a manual Drive folder, or Recovery Inbox.
Protect the evidence: Never clear site data or delete a local walk while recovery is pending.

Before Leaving

  • Microphone capture verified.
  • Every room selected and announced; bedrooms and bathrooms numbered consistently.
  • Overview and detail photos captured for material conditions.
  • Narration and room labels checked in Review.
  • Destination confirmed and status reads Uploaded & Processed.
Remember

Review Walk does not upload. Use Resume Walk if anything is missing. End Walk & Upload only when the session is complete.

Recorder Workflow

  • START: Select property, evaluator, Process, and Visit Type. Registered properties sync when Start a New Walk opens; tap refresh if one is missing.
  • UNREGISTERED: Select Other → Unregistered Property Quick Capture, type the address or location, and begin. This creates a temporary Capture ID, not a Property ID.
  • NARRATE: State the address, visit type, current location, visible condition, uncertainty, and who should follow up.
  • PAUSE: Pause for phone calls or unrelated conversation; resume before continuing.
  • PHOTOGRAPH: Take an overview, useful opposite view, and condition details while naming the subject. Use available .5×, 1×, 2×, or 3× zoom controls.
  • RETURN: In the camera, use Done or Done — Return to Recording. Do not close the Recorder with the browser X.
  • REVIEW: Tap Review Walk. Check every narration segment and photo. Nothing uploads yet.
  • RESUME: Tap Resume Walk to add narration or photos to the same Walk ID, then return to Review Walk.
  • UPLOAD: Tap End Walk & Upload, confirm, and keep the Recorder open until it says Uploaded & Processed.
  • RECOVER: In My Walks, use Continue Walk, Review Captured, Resume Walk, or Retry Upload according to the displayed status.
Protect the evidence: Do not clear browser site data or delete the walk before Drive confirms Uploaded & Processed. If upload pauses, reconnect—preferably on strong Wi-Fi—and tap Retry Upload.

Narration Pattern

  • START: Full address or location, date, visit type, and starting area.
  • MOVE: “Now reviewing…” or “Now entering…” plus a clear current-use area name.
  • DESCRIBE: Orientation, floor, ceiling/walls, openings, fixtures, visible scope, and uncertainty.
  • FLAG: “Evidence gap…” plus who should resolve it.
  • CORRECT: “Correction…” or “Resolution… this supersedes my earlier concern.”
  • CLOSE: Summarize open, resolved, and optional items; state the full address or location again.
Operating Standard: Say where you are. Describe what you see. State what is known, what is uncertain, and whether an earlier concern has been resolved.
